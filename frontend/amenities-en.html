Common questions about amenities and services

How big are the apartments and how many guests do they sleep?

Each apartment is 220 square metres with 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, a separate living room and a guest room, and sleeps 7 to 8 guests.

Is parking available?

Yes. Each apartment has at least one dedicated parking space. Parking is indoor and secured, with smart access to the garage, and some apartments have more than one space.

Is housekeeping free?

Yes. Regular housekeeping is free for guests, usually every other day, and includes tidying the rooms, making the beds, changing towels and replenishing bathroom supplies. Extra cleaning is available for a fee.

What kind of internet do the apartments have?

High-speed fiber internet, with a separate Wi-Fi network and a private password for each apartment, and full coverage in every room.

Is there heating and air conditioning?

Yes. Every apartment has central heating, plus 5 modern air conditioners that provide both cooling and heating.

What is the minimum stay and when is check-out?

The minimum stay is 3 nights. Standard check-out is at 12:00 noon, and a late check-out of a few hours can be requested in advance, subject to availability. Check-in is by prior arrangement.

Are smoking and pets allowed?

Smoking and pets are allowed in selected apartments only. Ask us before booking and we will suggest a suitable apartment.

Do you provide a baby cot or an extra bed?

Yes, free on request: a baby cot, an extra bed, an extra mattress, and extra towels, pillows and duvets.

Do you offer airport pick-up or transfers?

Yes. Airport pick-up and transfers to and from the airport can be arranged for an extra charge.

Is there a washing machine in the apartment?

Yes. Every apartment has a separate laundry room with a washing machine, drying rack, iron and laundry detergent. A laundry and ironing service is also available for an extra charge.

Are the buildings wheelchair accessible?

Yes. All buildings have elevators and wheelchair-friendly entrances.

Are the apartments for families?

Yes, the apartments are for families only.
