About Jordan Hospital

Jordan Hospital is a private, multi-specialty hospital on Queen Noor Street in the Shmeisani area of Amman.

On its website, the hospital states that it is accredited by Joint Commission International (JCI) and by Jordan's Health Care Accreditation Council (HCAC).

Why stay close to the hospital?

Treatment means repeated trips: tests, results, follow-ups and sometimes daily visits to a patient who has been admitted. Staying in Shmeisani itself cuts travel time and fatigue, and keeps you close if you need to get back to the hospital quickly.

An apartment that works for patients and companions

  • Three bedrooms let the patient rest in a quiet room while companions have their own rooms.
  • The kitchen makes it easy to prepare meals that follow the doctor's instructions or a required diet.
  • The living room gives the family space to relax instead of spending weeks in a small hotel room.

Tips before you arrive

  • Book your stay once your first hospital appointment is confirmed, and extend if treatment takes longer.
  • For stays longer than a month, ask us about the long-stay discount.
  • Keep the hospital and clinic numbers with you and confirm appointments with them directly.
