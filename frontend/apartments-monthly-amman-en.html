Why a monthly furnished rental?

A yearly lease means a long contract, furniture and setting up utilities. With a monthly furnished apartment you arrive with your suitcase: furniture, kitchen and internet are already there, and you book only the time you need.

Who is a monthly rental for?

  • Patients on long treatment in Amman, and their companions.
  • Employees and consultants on assignments lasting several months.
  • Families spending the summer or a transition period before settling.
  • Expats returning home for a long visit.

What you get

  • 3 bedrooms, including one master, and 3 bathrooms.
  • A fully equipped kitchen in the apartment and high-speed fiber internet.
  • A balcony in every apartment, and two in some.
  • A Shmeisani location close to Abdali, hospitals, restaurants and services.
