Furnished Apartments for Medical Stays in Amman

Many patients travel to Amman for treatment from across Jordan, the Gulf and other Arab countries. HIJAZI Apartments in Shmeisani give them and their companions a whole apartment close to hospitals in Shmeisani and Abdali, for days or months.

In short

  • Fully furnished apartments in Shmeisani, Amman.
  • 3 bedrooms in every apartment, including one master, and 3 bathrooms.
  • A kitchen in every apartment, plus Wi-Fi.
  • Most apartments have two balconies; one apartment has one balcony.
  • Daily, weekly or monthly stays, with a special discount for stays longer than one month.
  • Book directly on the website or through WhatsApp.

What patients and companions need from a stay

  • Quiet, and a private room where the patient can rest between appointments.
  • A kitchen to prepare food that fits a diet or the doctor's instructions.
  • Space for companions instead of sharing one hotel room.
  • Flexibility to extend if treatment takes longer.
  • Our apartments have 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms and a kitchen, and stays can be extended subject to availability.

How to plan a medical stay in Amman

  • Confirm your first appointment with the hospital and ask how long treatment is expected to take.
  • Book your stay for that period and extend later if needed.
  • For more than a month, ask us about the long-stay discount.
  • Message us on WhatsApp before you arrive to arrange your check-in.
