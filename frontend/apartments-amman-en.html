Why stay in Shmeisani?

Shmeisani is one of Amman's best-known business and residential districts, with banks, offices, restaurants, cafés and everyday shops. Staying here keeps most of what you need close by.

It sits next to Abdali, Amman's modern downtown with The Boulevard and Abdali Mall, so you get a residential neighbourhood with easy access to the city centre.
